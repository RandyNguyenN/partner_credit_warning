Customer Credit Warning

Give each customer a credit limit. When their outstanding balance goes over it, the Sale Order shows a red banner and Confirm either asks for a second thought (Warn) or is refused (Block).

Free | Free · LGPL-3 · Set a limit on the customer and you are done
😓The Problem
  • ✗ Salespeople confirm new orders for customers who already owe you more than you are willing to carry.
  • ✗ The customer's open balance sits in Invoicing — nobody looks at it while writing a quotation.
  • ✗ Some customers only need a reminder, others must not get a new order at all — one rule for all does not fit.
  • ✗ Finance only finds out at reconciliation time, after the goods have left.
🎯The Solution
  • ✓ Credit Limit per customer in the Sales & Purchase tab — 0 means no limit.
  • ✓ Red banner on the Sale Order as soon as an over-limit customer is selected.
  • ✓ Warn or Block per customer — a Confirm Anyway / Cancel dialog, or a hard error that stops confirmation.
  • ✓ Credit Status in the Other Info tab shows Current Debt next to the Credit Limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Current Debt" include?
It is Odoo's own receivable balance of the customer company (the same total receivable Invoicing computes): posted invoices not yet paid, minus unreconciled payments and credit notes. Draft invoices and the order you are confirming are not included.
What's the difference between Warning and Blocked?
Warning: Confirm opens a dialog showing the debt and the limit; the salesperson can Confirm Anyway or Cancel. Blocked: Confirm shows an error and the order stays a quotation until the balance drops under the limit or the limit is changed.
Can a manager override a Blocked customer?
Not from the order — Block applies to every user. To let an order through, change the customer to Warning or raise the credit limit.
How is this different from the Customer Credit Hold app?
This free app compares the total open balance with a credit limit you set per customer. Customer Credit Hold looks at overdue invoices instead (overdue days and amount thresholds) and adds Sales Manager approval with an audit log.
Does it change my existing data?
No. It adds two fields on customers — Credit Limit (default 0 = no limit) and On Limit Exceeded (default Warning) — plus computed display fields on the Sale Order. Nothing is blocked until you set a limit on a customer.
Does it work with Odoo Community?
Yes. It runs on Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise and depends only on Sales and Invoicing.
